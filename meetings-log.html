Table 1.1 Game ideas proposed prior to meeting. Refer to Game Idea Draft for further details.

Explanation of Game Idea 1

Tan Owen has provided his explanations of the general gameplay of game idea 1 as the following:

The game shall be educating players on photography skills. Players will be placed in levels of certain areas with fixed lighting, with the goal to capture photos with the appropriate camera settings and composition. To do so, the player can adjust the camera settings and manage photography compositions.

Explanation of Game Idea 2

Josh Leong Wing Seong has provided his explanations of the general gameplay of game idea 2 as the following:

The game shall revolve around the Chinese interior Feng Shui culture. Players will be placed into levels resembling a fully furnished house interior, with the goal to spot violations of interiors according to the interior Feng Shui rules, and suggest the most appropriate adjustments to the spot. To do so, the player can explore within the housing and be given options to make adjustments by selecting certain furniture.

Tan Owen has given a similar feedback of Game Idea 2 that the concepts require further specifications to prevent complications.

Discussion of Game Ideas

During the meeting, Wam Wei Kang has proposed two ideas that could be implemented into the game to expand the game's features and enhance the player's immersive experience.

Equipment Slots

The idea is that the GUI of the main gameplay screen will contain a toolbar that shows the camera equipment and the currently equipped ones (e.g., toolbars in Lucid Block).

This is a functionality-based feature that helps players navigate through various equipment quickly without breaking the flow of perfecting the photography. The toolbar will be placed at the bottom of the screen.

The idea suggests camera equipment features such as tripods and different varieties of lenses, to provide more options for players to take their perfect idea of photography or to complete the tasks. However, it is identified that the implementation is optional due to short deadlines and heavy workload, the team deemed it wise to hold off implementing the idea and advised to revise at later stages.

FPS POV Hand Implementation

An idea proposed by Wam Wei Kang that during the gameplay, players could see their "hand" and their actions trigger animations of the hand model to suggest their player character has truly moved and carried out the action.

The team decides that it is an interesting idea that enhances the player's immersive experience, which could be one of the attractions of the game. Since the implementation of the feature is rather small and simple in scale, the team agrees to implement the idea when the game begins development and Wei Kang has volunteered to be the responsible programmer for this.

The source of the hand model and its animation will be filmed by our team, with Tan Owen being responsible for filming and being the subject of film.
