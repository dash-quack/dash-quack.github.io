Week 1 • Practical 001

The Birth of Our Game: Formation & Theme Confirmation

Attendance: Tan Owen (Leader), Josh Leong Wing Seong, Wam Wei Kang (100% Present)

Practical Venue: Game Design Lab / Virtual Studio Session

Session Minutes & Decisions:

  • Formed 3-member group: Studio Dash Quack. Appointed Tan Owen as Group Leader.
  • Confirmed assigned theme: Educational (focused on teaching photography composition and the exposure triangle).
  • Brainstormed 3 initial proposals (Quiz app vs on-rails photo game vs 3D first-person photography simulation). Selected 3D simulation Ready, Frame, Shoot!.
  • Drafted official Game Concept Card establishing high concept, platform (PC/Unity), and core features.
  • Applied Topic 1's 7 Structural Elements of e-Games (Rules, Goals, Interaction, Challenges, Feedback, Outcomes, Representation).
  • Collected and cataloged 11 visual reference screenshots covering camera optics, composition, Unity terrain, low-poly aesthetics, and viewmodels.
✓ Practical 001 Checkpoint Outcome: Educational theme and photography simulation concept verified. The 7 structural elements and exposure triangle mechanics approved for Assignment 1 documentation.
