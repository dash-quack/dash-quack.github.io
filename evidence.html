Week 1 • Wednesday, 4 November 2026 • Practical 001

The Birth of Our Game: Formation & Theme Confirmation

Attendance: Alex Tan (Leader), Bryan Lee, Chloe Wong, David Ng (100% Present)

Practical Venue: Game Design Lab / Virtual Studio Session

Session Minutes & Decisions:

  • Formed 4-member group: Studio Dash Quack. Elected Alex Tan as Group Leader.
  • Confirmed assigned theme: Culture Game (focused on Malaysian folklore, Wayang Kulit, and Keris spiritual lore).

  • Applied Topic 1's 7 Structural Elements of e-Games (Rules, Goals, Interaction, Challenges, Feedback, Outcomes, Representation).
  • Initialized GitHub Pages digital portfolio repository: dash-quack.github.io.

Week 2 • Wednesday, 11 November 2026 • Practical 002

How Our Game Works: Systems, Controls & UI Architecture

Attendance: Alex Tan, Bryan Lee, Chloe Wong, David Ng (100% Present)

Practical Venue: Game Design Lab / Virtual Studio Session

Session Minutes & Decisions:

  • Formulated behavioral psychology mechanics: mapped Positive/Negative Reinforcement and 4 Operant Conditioning schedules (FR, VR, FI, VI).
  • Constructed the Rewards and Penalties Matrix detailing gameplay and economic impact of actions.
  • Outlined folklore 3-act narrative and embedded diegetic storytelling elements (petrified warriors of Sang Kelembai, batik frescoes).
  • Designed the Wayang Shadow Projection Puzzle geometry and gate-and-key state machine.
  • Mapped ergonomic Dual-Input Control Scheme (Xbox/DualShock Gamepad + Keyboard & Mouse).
  • Created the Minimalist Diegetic HUD Wireframe incorporating the Lotus health arc and floating compass bar.
✓ Practical 002 Checkpoint Outcome: Tutor verified the operant conditioning matrix and praised the ergonomic allocation of combat buttons to shoulder triggers.
Week 3 • Wednesday, 18 November 2026 • Practical 003

Designing the Gameplay: Spatial Layout, Flow & Tuning

Attendance: Alex Tan, Bryan Lee, Chloe Wong, David Ng (100% Present)

Practical Venue: Game Design Lab / Virtual Studio Session

Session Minutes & Decisions:

  • Standardized character physical metrics: walk (3.5 m/s), sprint (7.0 m/s), jump height (2.2m), jump distance (4.5m), and doorway clearance (3.0m).
  • Drafted top-down architectural level blueprint for Chapter 1: The Sunken Ruins of Santubong (Zones 1 to 4).
  • Mapped 25-minute mission tension curve applying Csikszentmihalyi's Flow Channel model to balance challenge against boredom/anxiety.
  • Calculated mathematical combat tuning tables (dodge i-frames = 0.25s, parry window = 0.18s, stamina recharge formula).
  • Established 3D asset budget: polycounts (hero: 20k tris), PBR texture maps (RMA packing), and vertical audio stem layers.
✓ Practical 003 Checkpoint Outcome: Tutor approved the top-down level blueprint and validated the mathematical tuning calculations for stamina and parry timing.
Week 4 • Wednesday, 25 November 2026 • Practical 004

Improving Our Game Design: Critique, Iteration & Final Synthesis

Attendance: Alex Tan, Bryan Lee, Chloe Wong, David Ng (100% Present)

Practical Venue: Game Design Lab / Virtual Studio Session

Session Minutes & Decisions:

  • Conducted peer review workshop with neighboring student studios; collected feedback on combat feel and puzzle clarity.
  • Executed 4 major design iterations with Before/After comparisons:
    • Widened parry timing from 0.10s to 0.18s and added golden spark telegraphs.
    • Elevated Mount Santubong Spire landmark and added glowing firefly breadcrumbs.
    • Replaced bulky rectangular HUD with diegetic Lotus arc and top-center compass bar.
    • Added harmonic audio and haptic rumble proximity feedback to the Wayang shadow puzzle.
  • Compiled final Game Design Document (GDD) executive summary for future 3D Game Programming implementation.
  • Audited website portfolio on GitHub Pages, verifying mobile responsiveness, accessibility, and broken link checks.
✓ Practical 004 Checkpoint Outcome: Final design portfolio approved by tutor. Certified ready for official coursework submission ahead of the Week 5 deadline.
