1.1 Group Formation & Theme Confirmation

Assigned Theme: Culture Game

During the Week 1 practical session, our group formed Studio Dash Quack comprising 4 members. We confirmed our assigned theme: Culture Game.

We chose the Culture Game theme because Malaysian and Southeast Asian folklore remains vastly underrepresented in the 3D action-adventure space. By drawing from living traditional arts—specifically Wayang Kulit (traditional shadow puppetry), the craftsmanship and spiritual lore of the Keris, and indigenous folklore like Puteri Santubong and Sang Kelembai—we saw a compelling opportunity to merge evocative storytelling with innovative light-and-shadow gameplay mechanics.

Cultural Inspiration Sources & Moodboard

1. Wayang Kulit Kelantan

The ancient art of bovine leather shadow puppetry projected against a cloth screen by an oil lamp. Inspires our core environmental puzzle mechanic: rotating 3D historical artifacts under directional sunlight to cast matching silhouette keys onto ancient stone doors.

2. The Spiritual Keris Blade

The asymmetrical dagger forged from folded celestial iron and nickel, renowned in Malay culture for its distinct wavy blade (lok) and pamor damascus patterns. Serves as Arya's living ancestral weapon capable of absorbing and refracting spiritual sunlight.

3. Folkloric Architecture & Bestiary

Traditional Rumah Tiang Dua Belas timber stilt joinery, carved floral motifs (Awan Larat), and mythical guardians: corrupted Garuda avian spirits and stone-petrified ruins influenced by the Sang Kelembai legend.

Industry Reference Games

We analyzed how global cultural action-adventure titles successfully translate indigenous art styles into compelling 3D gameplay:

Benchmark Game Cultural Foundation Key Design Takeaway for Hikayat
Raji: An Ancient Epic Ancient Indian mythology & Pahari miniature painting How to integrate traditional religious murals and architectural storytelling seamlessly into level backgrounds.
Kena: Bridge of Spirits Balinese and East Asian animist spirituality Pacing balance between charming spiritual exploration, environmental puzzles, and tight, high-stakes melee combat.
Ghost of Tsushima Feudal Japanese Samurai cinema Diegetic environmental guidance (guiding wind, yellow songbirds) over intrusive HUD markers, reinforcing cultural immersion.
Okami Japanese Shinto folklore & Sumi-e ink wash How a distinct traditional art technique (the Celestial Brush) can serve as both an expressive combat tool and a world puzzle solver.

1.2 Idea Exploration & Divergent Brainstorming

Design Evolution

To ensure a thorough design exploration, our studio brainstormed three divergent game concepts during the Week 1 practical before converging on our final direction.

Concept Proposal Core Gameplay Hook Strengths Weaknesses & Elimination Rationale
Proposal A: "Wau: Wind Rider"
Genre: Flight Exploration Simulator		 Player controls an oversized Malaysian kite (Wau Bulan) riding coastal thermals to deliver spiritual offerings between islands. Relaxing pacing; strong visual novelty; authentic traditional kite motifs. Lacked dynamic player interaction models; difficult to construct diverse challenges; limited combat and tuning depth required for CLO2.
Proposal B: "Bayang: Shadow Weaver"
Genre: 2D Shadow Puzzle Platformer		 A 2.5D puzzle platformer where the player walks on 2D shadows cast by 3D background objects (similar to Contrast). Deep puzzle potential; direct homage to Wayang Kulit cloth screens. Did not fulfill the assignment requirement for a rich 3D game design with 3D spatial navigation and third-person camera systems.
Proposal C (Selected): "Hikayat: Echoes of the Keris"
Genre: 3D Cultural Action-Adventure		 Full 3D third-person adventure combining Silat martial combat with 3D light-reflection Wayang puzzles and ancestral spirit restoration. Exceptional synergy with CLO2 (control schemes, spatial 3D flow, combat tuning); rich narrative lore; scalable for 3D Game Programming. Selected: We retained Proposal B's brilliant shadow-manipulation concept and elevated it into full 3D spatial environmental puzzle chambers!

🔄 How Our Design Changed During Week 1

Initially, our group debated between a peaceful 2D shadow puzzle game and a high-speed flying game. During our practical tutor consultation, we realized that an avatar-based 3D action-adventure provided the richest canvas to demonstrate CLO2 (interaction models, ergonomic controller schemes, spatial level flow, and tuning parameters). Consequently, we merged Proposal B's Wayang shadow puzzles as the mental challenge pillar of Proposal C's 3D world.

High-Concept Elevator Pitch

Hikayat: Echoes of the Keris is a 3D single-player cultural action-adventure where players step into the sandals of Arya, a spirited young silat apprentice who awakens the ancient Spirit of the Keris Taming Sari. As a supernatural eclipse corrupts the sacred highlands of Mount Santubong, players must master fluid silat martial arts, align sacred Wayang shadow puppets to solve ancestral temple riddles, and restore harmony between mortals and celestial folklore guardians.

The Three Gameplay Pillars

  1. Light & Shadow Puzzle Solving: Wielding the reflective pamor blade to channel beams of sunlight, manipulating physical Wayang screens to unlock sealed sanctums.
  2. Rhythmic Silat Martial Melee: Evasive steps (langkah), defensive parries, and directional slashes tuned for rewarding timing and responsiveness.
  3. Living Southeast Asian Mythology: Discovering forgotten folklore murals, experiencing diegetic gamelan musical reactivity, and cleansing corrupted mythic beasts.

1.4 Application of Topic 1: The 7 Structural Elements of e-Games

Core Theoretical Framework

As outlined in Topic 1 (Overview of Computer Game Design), all electronic games are constructed upon seven fundamental structural elements. Below, we articulate how each element is manifested in Hikayat.

1. Rules

The governing constraints that structure player actions and world behavior.

  • Operational Rules: Moving with WASD / Left-Stick; dodging consumes 20% stamina; parrying requires timing within a 0.18-second window before an enemy impact.
  • Foundational Rules: The underlying mathematics governing hitpoints (100 HP max), physical collision bounding boxes, and shadow-casting projection rays calculated via light raycasts.
  • Behavioral Rules: Fair play principles, single-player agency, checkpoint saving without permanent loss of unlocked cultural relics.

2. Goals

The motivational objectives that provide purpose and forward momentum.

  • Ultimate Goal: Ascend the summit of Mount Santubong, cleanse the Corrupted Garuda, and restore the Solar Solstice to dissipate the shadow curse.
  • Short-Term Goals: Unlock the sealed stone gate of Zone 3 by solving the Wayang light alignment puzzle.
  • Moment-to-Moment Goals: Successfully parry a shadow minion's slash; locate the nearest Spirit Brazier to recharge health.

3. Interaction

The communication loop between the player and the game world.

  • Avatar Direct Control: Third-person over-the-shoulder responsive navigation with smooth kinematic transitions (running, climbing, vaulting).
  • World Manipulation: Rotating antique copper mirrors to refract light beams across overgrown temple ruins; lighting incense braziers.
  • Cultural NPC Interaction: Conversing with the Dalang (Master Puppeteer) spirit at waypoints to receive cryptic folklore hints.

4. Challenges

Obstacles that require player skill, knowledge, and problem-solving to overcome.

  • Spatial Navigation: Scaling decaying stilt-house scaffolding and leap across ravine ledges without falling into shadow brambles.
  • Cognitive / Puzzle: Deciphering Wayang shadow silhouettes by identifying which mythic animal shape corresponds to ancient mural inscriptions.
  • Dexterity & Reflexes: Reacting to fast telegraphed attacks from multiple shadow hounds while managing stamina reserves.

5. Feedback

Sensory cues informing the player about the state of their actions.

  • Visual: Golden spark bloom upon a successful parry; screen borders darken with shadow vignette as health drops below 25%.
  • Auditory: Resonant gamelan gong chime when a puzzle element clicks into place; dynamic ethnic flute crescendo during combat.
  • Haptic / Kinesthetic: Controller motor vibration pulses when standing near concealed ancestral pamor ore.

6. Outcomes

The consequences and quantifiable results of player performance.

  • Success State: Cleansing a sanctum brings radiant daylight back to the zone, dispels creeping shadow barriers, and unlocks new Silat stances.
  • Failure State: Health reaches 0 -> Arya collapses, returning to the nearest incense checkpoint with enemies reset, but completed puzzle states preserved.
  • Progression Rating: Lore completion percentage and spiritual keris purity ranking at the end of each chapter.

7. Representation

The expressive audio-visual and conceptual framing of the virtual world.

Visual Aesthetics

Stylized PBR 3D rendering. Warm golden sunlight piercing through dense tropical canopies, contrasted against misty deep indigo shadow miasma. Traditional Kelantanese floral batik carvings adorn temple pillars.

Acoustic Design

Diegetic live recordings of Malaysian gamelan, rebab (two-stringed spiked fiddle), and serunai (reed wind instrument) dynamically cross-faded using vertical orchestration layers based on combat intensity.

Thematic Narrative

A celebration of cultural preservation, balance between light and dark, and humility in ancestral heritage rather than sheer destructive dominance.
