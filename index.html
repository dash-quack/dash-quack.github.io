Executive Game Concept: Hikayat

3D Cultural Action-Adventure

The Premise

In an ancient realm veiled in perpetual eclipse, an apprentice warrior named Arya discovers the fractured Keris Taming Sari, an ancestral spirit blade imbued with the power to refract light and dispel shadows. Guided by the celestial spirit of the keris, Arya must traverse the sanctums of Mount Santubong, unravel light-and-shadow puppetry puzzles, and cleanse the mythic creatures corrupted by shadow dissonance.

Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)

  • Wayang Light-and-Shadow Mechanics: Manipulate sacred braziers and antique relics to project folklore shadow silhouettes that physically manipulate level architecture.
  • Authentic Silat Martial Combat: Fluid 3D melee combat incorporating defensive parries, evasive langkah footwork, and spiritual keris attunement.
  • Rich Southeast Asian Cultural Immersion: Diegetic gamelan acoustics, intricate batik motifs, and authentic mythological bestiaries (Garuda, Sang Kelembai).
